## Why
The studio uses tiny tracked capital labels, decorative numbering, repeated promotional copy, and a patterned canvas. These compete with the photograph and make a practical editing tool feel like a landing page.

## What Changes
Refine only the studio: neutral work surfaces, readable control labels, a concise upload state, and restrained preset rows. Preserve the existing brand and every editing operation. Retain responsive panels, bounded preset scrolling, collapsible categories, instant previews and lazy backend preset retrieval.

## Capabilities
### Modified Capabilities
- `studio-interface`: clarify hierarchy and remove ornamental content.

## Impact
Studio.tsx and Studio.module.css only. No backend, home page or shared navigation changes.
